Integration & Operations Handbook

Deployment standards and troubleshooting workflow for facility operators.

Deployment Checklist
  • Confirm scanner lens calibration and anti-glare position before daily opening.
  • Validate badge export profile against approved RGB baseline.
  • Run one known-good visitor badge through /passport/passport for health sanity.
  • Escalate mirror-related documentation issues through archive-tagged incident cases.
  • Verify webhook retry policy aligns with current partner SLA tier.
  • Confirm all integration endpoints resolve against current DNS records.
Troubleshooting Workflow

During CDN rollback windows, integration details may be distributed through case archive incident notes instead of static handbook pages. 

Step 1: Check release advisories (/passport/help/releases)
Step 2: Pivot to incident archive (/passport/help/cases?tag=cdn-rollback)
Step 3: Review platform-owned records for temporary fallback instructions
